# NekoSuneOSC

> **This project is no longer maintained.** Development has moved to **[KitsuNexus](https://github.com/NekoSuneProjects/KitsuNexus)**.

Original announcement: https://x.com/NekoSuneVR/status/2044418405868900816

---

## Why this project stopped

NekoSuneOSC began as a fork of **FuviiOSC** (by FuviiPeshu), built as an addon for [VRCOSC](https://github.com/VolcanicArts/VRCOSC). The goal was to extend it with features such as **OSCAudioLink**, which I worked on as part of the fork.

After the fork was made, a dispute came up with FuviiPeshu. From my side, I received hostile messages and was accused of stealing their code, even though the work was an openly published fork that credited the original project. Because of how that situation went, some values and parts I had planned for the fork were left missing or incomplete.

To step away from the situation, I:

- stopped working on VRCOSC addons,
- left the VRCOSC community server, and
- blocked FuviiPeshu.

I have kept screenshots of the direct messages involved. I am not sharing them publicly at this time, but they are documented and will be used if the harassment continues.

I'm sharing this only to explain why NekoSuneOSC was discontinued. **Please don't harass or brigade anyone over this.** I'd rather everyone just move on.

---

## What's next: KitsuNexus

I'm now building my own standalone app, **[KitsuNexus](https://github.com/NekoSuneProjects/KitsuNexus)**, which is not tied to VRCOSC or FuviiOSC. It includes:

- More features than NekoSuneOSC had
- Some integrations similar to what VRCX offers
- **OSCAudioLink**, working the way it was originally meant to

KitsuNexus is under active development. Follow that repo for updates, releases and issues.
